Straight answers

Common questions.

How fast will I start seeing results?

Most clients see their Google ranking and call volume go up within the first 30 days. The Growth plan comes with a guarantee on this — if nothing moves in month 1, we work month 2 free. Results keep building over 3 to 6 months as everything compounds.

Do I need to run ads?

No. This whole system runs without ads. SEO, your Google Business Profile, and Yelp all work through organic search — people who are already looking for what you do find you first. That makes the leads better quality than cold ad traffic, and it doesn't stop the moment you pause a campaign.

What's the difference between the $197 and $497 plan?

The $197 plan gives you the website, SEO, Yelp management, missed-call text-back, and review automation. The $497 plan adds full Google Business Profile management — which is the biggest driver of Maps 3-Pack calls — plus reputation management, database reactivation, monthly reports, priority access, and the first-month guarantee. If you're serious about growing, the $497 plan is where the real results are.

I get the leads — but do you close them for me?

No, and we're upfront about that. We get the right people calling your number. People who searched for your service, found you, and chose to reach out. Closing those calls into paying clients is on you — pick up the phone, be helpful, and do your job. We make sure your phone rings.

Why only 10 clients per month?

Because we actually do the work ourselves. Not a software tool, not a template system — real people working on your accounts. Capping at 10 keeps quality high and makes sure every client we take on actually sees movement. When those spots fill up, the next opening is next month.

Can I cancel, and what happens to my website?

You can cancel anytime — one click, no notice needed, no fee. If you cancel, the website goes offline since we host it. Your domain is yours forever no matter what — we transfer it to you for free if you ever leave.
