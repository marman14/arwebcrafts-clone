Straight Answers

Common Questions Answered.

How quickly will I start seeing results?

Most clients start seeing measurable improvement in Google rankings and lead volume within the first 30 days. Full results compound over 3–6 months as domain authority, GBP ranking, and Yelp positioning strengthen. The Growth and Annual plans include a first-month results guarantee — if you don't see movement, we work the next month free.

Do I need to spend on ads?

No. Our entire system is built around organic lead generation — SEO, Google Business Profile, and Yelp rankings. You will not need to spend a dollar on Facebook ads, Google ads, or any other paid traffic. The leads come from people actively searching for your services in your area — which makes them far higher quality than cold ad traffic anyway.

Will you generate leads for me, or clients?

We generate inbound leads and phone calls. Real people searching for your service who find you and reach out. Converting those leads into paying clients is your job — you answer the phone, do the consultation, and close. We handle everything that happens before they contact you: visibility, rankings, and trust signals.

What's the difference between the $197 and $497 plans?

The $197/month Starter plan covers your SEO website, monthly SEO updates, Yelp optimization, and hosting. The $497/month Growth plan adds full Google Business Profile management (the biggest driver of local Map Pack calls), reputation management, missed-call text-back, monthly ranking reports, and our first-month results guarantee. If you're serious about growth, the Growth plan is where the real results live.

Why only 10 clients per month?

Because we actually do the work. We're a team of real people, not an automated system. To ensure every client gets real results and genuine attention, we strictly cap new onboardings at 10 per month. When those 10 slots fill up, the next opening is the following month. This keeps quality high and prevents us from overpromising.

Can I cancel, and what happens to my domain?

You can cancel your subscription anytime with one click — no contracts, no cancellation fees, no notice period required. Your domain is 100% yours forever. We transfer it to you for free if you ever leave. The website itself goes offline if you cancel (since we host it), but you keep your domain and all rankings you've built.

Do you work with health & wellness businesses specifically?

Absolutely. We work extensively with medical clinics, dental offices, med spas, chiropractic practices, mental health therapists, senior care facilities, fitness studios, and health coaches. We understand patient privacy considerations and how to write copy that builds trust with patients — not just generic SEO content.
