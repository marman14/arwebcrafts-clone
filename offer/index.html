Straight answers

What exactly gets charged today?

99 if you pay monthly, or 799 if you pay for the year. That is it. No setup fee, no development fee, no deposit, no design fee. You can see and cancel the subscription yourself from a link in your receipt.

Really no setup fee? Everyone else charges one.

Really none. Most shops charge two to five thousand to build and then hand you the keys. We would rather charge nothing to build and keep you for two years, because that only works if the site actually brings you work. If it does not, you cancel and we have lost, which is the right way round.

Is 24 hours real?

Yes, and here is the honest version of it. The clock starts when your form and your photos reach us, not when you pay. Send everything at 4pm and you are looking at your site the next afternoon. Send three photos and no service area and we will be chasing you, and that is the part that turns 24 hours into a week for some people.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. No contract, no minimum, no notice period. There is a link in your receipt that takes you to your own billing page and you cancel it there yourself. No email to write and no phone call to sit through.

What happens to my site if I cancel?

It comes down. This is a service, not a one off build, which is the whole reason there is no setup fee. We carry the cost of building the site and we earn it back over time, so the site runs while you are subscribed and stops when you stop. Same as Squarespace or Shopify, except we do the work instead of you.

Your domain is different. That stays yours, we transfer it over at no charge, and nobody can point it anywhere without you. And if you ever want to own the site outright and move it somewhere else, say so and we will quote you a price to buy it.

Whose domain is it?

Yours. We register it and manage it as part of the 99 so there is nothing for you to renew, nothing to forget and no second bill. If you ever leave, we transfer it to you at no charge and you keep it. That is written into what you agree to at checkout, not something you have to take my word for.

What if I do not like what you build?

Unlimited changes, as many rounds as it takes. If it is still not right, cancel and we refund the month. You keep the domain either way.

How fast do edits actually happen?

Small things the same day. A new phone number, a price change, swapping photos, adding a service. Anything bigger, like a new page or a seasonal offer with its own landing page, inside two business days. You text or email, we do it, no form to fill in and no hourly bill.

I already have a website. Is that a problem?

No, it is usually easier. We rebuild it and move everything across, same price, and nothing goes dark in between. Wix, GoDaddy, Squarespace, WordPress, an old one nobody has the login for any more. We have moved all of those.

How do I know the site is bringing in work?

Ask for call tracking when you sign up and we put a tracked number on the site. Then once a month you get one page showing calls, form fills and which pages they came from. It is free on both plans, and it is the only way either of us can tell whether this is worth the money.

Who actually builds it?

A team of six developers, all senior. We are based in Pakistan and we work US hours, so when you text during your day somebody answers the same day. You deal with Arman for everything, not a support queue.

Will you keep calling me?

No. If you buy, we build. If you ask for the free demo, we contact you once to confirm and once with the link, and that is it. Reply STOP and you never hear from us again.
