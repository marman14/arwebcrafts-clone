Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Custom WordPress Themes

Transparent answers to common questions about our theme engineering process, timelines, Gutenberg support, and ownership.

What are the benefits of a custom WordPress theme over a pre-made template?

Pre-made commercial templates and multipurpose themes are built to cater to everyone, which means they come packed with dozens of external plugins, thousands of lines of unused CSS/JS, and rigid page builders that cripple your page load times and Core Web Vitals. A custom WordPress theme engineered by AR Webcrafts is built specifically for your business requirements. It contains zero unnecessary code, achieves sub-second load times, offers a tailored Gutenberg editing experience, and eliminates dependency vulnerabilities.

Can you convert our Figma, Adobe XD, or Sketch designs into a WordPress theme?

Yes, pixel-perfect design translation is our core specialty. We take your Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, or InVision prototypes and faithfully code them into semantic, responsive HTML5/CSS3 templates. We match all typography hierarchies, spacing grids, fluid responsive breakpoints, hover effects, and interactive animations with 100% precision.

Do you build themes using Gutenberg Full Site Editing (FSE) or page builders?

We specialize in modern WordPress Full Site Editing (FSE) and custom Gutenberg block development powered by theme.json. This provides your content team with an intuitive visual editing experience inside native WordPress without the heavy performance penalty of third-party builders like Elementor or Divi. However, if your existing workflow strictly requires a specific builder or ACF Flexible Content, we can engineer custom components tailored to your preferred stack.

How long does custom WordPress theme development typically take?

Timelines depend on project complexity and the number of unique template layouts. A standard bespoke business theme with 5 to 8 unique layouts typically takes 3 to 4 weeks from approved designs to staging deployment. Larger enterprise portals or custom WooCommerce storefronts with advanced filtering and dynamic micro-interactions generally take 5 to 7 weeks. We provide clear milestone schedules and weekly staging updates.

Will our custom theme be optimized for Google Core Web Vitals and SEO?

Absolutely. High performance and semantic SEO are engineered directly into our theme foundation. We implement lean CSS architectures, deferred script loading, native responsive image srcset attributes, clean schema.org microdata, and minimal DOM depth. Our themes consistently score 95+ on Google PageSpeed Insights and pass all Core Web Vitals metrics out of the box.

Who owns the source code once the theme development is finished?

You do—100%. Upon final project sign-off and payment, you receive complete, unencrypted ownership of all theme files, templates, styles, custom block code, and associated Git repository history. There are no ongoing licensing fees, no vendor lock-in, and no hidden subscriptions.

What post-launch support and warranty do you provide?

Every custom theme includes our complimentary 60-day post-launch warranty covering any bug fixes or compatibility adjustments on the production server. We also provide full documentation and loom video tutorials for your team, along with optional ongoing SLA maintenance retainers for continuous feature iterations and updates.
