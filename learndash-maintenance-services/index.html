LearnDash Maintenance Services

Your students are paying for access to your courses. When something breaks — a quiz won’t submit, a certificate won’t generate, a payment gateway stops syncing — they notice immediately and you hear about it. LearnDash maintenance isn’t just about keeping software updated. It’s about making sure the platform that runs your education business stays reliable.

We handle ongoing maintenance for LearnDash sites so that when WordPress drops a core update or a plugin conflicts with your setup, someone’s already checked it before your students hit the problem.

What’s included:
Monthly LearnDash, theme, and plugin updates with compatibility testing before deployment. Security scanning and hardening. Performance monitoring — course load times, quiz submission speed, video delivery. Bug fixes when something breaks. Integration checks for payment gateways, Zoom/webinar tools, and membership plugins connected to your site.

How it works:
We run all updates on a staging copy of your site first. If anything breaks in testing, we fix it before it ever touches your live platform. You get a monthly report showing what was updated, what was tested, and the current status of your site.

Who this is for:
Course creators running LearnDash as their primary business platform. Online education companies with active student enrollments. Anyone who can’t afford their LMS to go down mid-course.

Regular Updates & Upgrades

We perform routine updates to your LearnDash plugins, themes, and WordPress core to ensure compatibility with the latest versions and security patches. Our team monitors updates closely to avoid conflicts and disruptions, ensuring that your site remains current and fully functional.

Security Monitoring & Enhancements

Security is a top priority for any LearnDash site. We implement robust security measures to protect against potential threats and vulnerabilities. This includes regular scans for malware, security audits, and timely application of security patches to safeguard your site and data from possible breaches.

Performance Optimization

We continuously monitor and optimize your LearnDash website’s speed and performance to maintain a high-performing site. This includes optimizing page load times, enhancing database efficiency, and ensuring smooth user experiences across all devices. Our goal is to provide a seamless learning experience for your users without performance issues.

Content & Feature Updates

Keeping your educational content and features up-to-date is essential for engaging learners. We assist with updating course materials, adding new features, and making necessary adjustments to ensure your site reflects the latest educational trends and your evolving needs.

Backup & Recovery

Regular backups are crucial for data protection and recovery. We implement and manage automated backup solutions to ensure your site’s data is securely stored and easily recoverable in case of any issues. Our team is prepared to restore your site quickly during data loss or other emergencies.

Bug Fixes & Technical Support

Our maintenance services include prompt resolution of any technical issues or bugs that may arise. We provide responsive support to address and fix problems efficiently, ensuring minimal disruption to your site’s operations and the learning experience.

Performance Reporting & Recommendations

We provide regular reports on your site’s performance, security status, and any maintenance activities performed. Additionally, we offer recommendations for improvements or upgrades to help you optimize your LearnDash site further and effectively meet your educational goals.
