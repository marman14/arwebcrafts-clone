STEP 1: DISCOVERY CALL
(30 minutes, 100% free)

You talk directly with the developers who will build your project. We discuss:
– Your current situation and pain points
– Specific features or systems you need
– Technical requirements and constraints
– Timeline and budget

Within 48 hours: Detailed proposal with scope, timeline, and fixed pricing no surprise costs mid-project

STEP 2: KICKOFF & PLANNING
(Week 1)

Once you approve the proposal:
– Contract signed, 25% deposit to begin
– Trello board created for your project
– Requirements finalization and tech architecture
– Design mockups (if applicable)
– Development environment setup

You have: Direct Slack channel + weekly call schedule

STEP 3: DEVELOPMENT
(Weeks 2-10)

We build in phases, with your input throughout:

Week 2-4: Core functionality
Week 5-7: Advanced features
Week 8-9: Integration and testing
Week 10: Polish and bug fixes

Every week: 30-minute progress call
Your Trello board: Every commit pushed to staging environment so you see real-time progress, not just a demo after 6 weeks.

STEP 4: LAUNCH & HANDOFF
(Week 11-12)

We don’t just hand off and disappear:
– Staging environment testing
– Training session for your team (2 hours)
– Documentation (user guides + technical docs)
– Production deployment
– Post-launch monitoring

30-day post-launch support included. We stay on after launch because bugs always show up in production that didn’t show in testing.
