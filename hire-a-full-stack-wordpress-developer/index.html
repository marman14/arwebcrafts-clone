Frequently Asked Questions

When to hire a dedicated WordPress developer?

Hiring a full-time WordPress developer is the preferred option for agencies that have two or more new websites to be built every month, or a mix of projects and smaller tasks like maintenance and updates to existing sites.

You can also choose to send us WordPress development projects one-by-one that we quote with a fixed price, or use a combination of the two. It’s important to us that you always have the right capacity to meet your demand. Not more, not less.

If you prefer, you can start with a couple of fixed-price projects so we can learn your preferences and show you our work before you hire WordPress developer from us.

How to communicate with your WordPress developer?

You communicate directly with your WordPress developer using Skype, Slack or through your project management software. To make the relationship successful, it’s valuable for you as a partner to establish clear and open communication, just as you would with an internally hired developer.

What is the cost of hiring a WordPress developer?

The cost of hiring a WordPress developer depends on their experience level and the complexity of your project. Typically, you can expect:

Junior WordPress Developers: Around $999/month for basic WordPress skills, simple PHP functions, and familiarity with CSS and JavaScript.

Mid-Level WordPress Developers: About $1,999/month, capable of handling more complex tasks like WooCommerce setup, custom widget creation, and version control.

Senior WordPress Developers: Approximately $2,900/month, equipped with advanced expertise in REST APIs, custom plugin development, and creating tailored solutions for complex projects.

These rates are for full-time, dedicated developers (40 hours/week). Your budget should align with the scope and technical needs of your project to ensure the right level of expertise.

What is a dedicated developer?

A dedicated developer is a professional software developer who is hired exclusively to work on a specific project or for a particular company on a long-term basis.

Unlike freelancers who often juggle multiple clients, a dedicated developer focuses solely on the assigned tasks, becoming an integral part of the team. This model is commonly used in outsourcing arrangements where the developer is employed full-time by an agency or a client.
