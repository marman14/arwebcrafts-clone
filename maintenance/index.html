Frequently Ask Questions

What is WordPress maintenance?

WordPress maintenance includes tasks such as updating core files, plugins, and themes to ensure security and compatibility. This also includes regular backups, security monitoring, performance optimization and fixing minor bugs.

- Why is regular WordPress maintenance important?

Regular maintenance keeps your website secure, protects it from hacking attempts, optimizes performance for faster loading times, and prevents compatibility issues.

- How often should one update WordPress website?

We recommend updating core files, plugins and themes whenever new versions are available. Security updates are especially important for quick installation.

- Will my website experience downtime during maintenance?

In most cases, regular website maintenance does not result in any downtime. However, for larger updates or complex tasks, we will inform you in advance of any potential downtime.

- Can I handle WordPress maintenance myself?

Yes, it is possible to manage maintenance yourself. However, this requires technical knowledge and may be time-consuming. That’s why we offer very reasonable packages for our services, so you can focus on running your business, and we take care of the security and smooth operation of your website.

-Can you host my website?

While our basic service focuses on website maintenance, but we also provide website hosting services if you require. Please check our detailed plans for details.

- Can you do a "specific task" for me?

Our maintenance plans cover a wide range of tasks, but if you have a specific request that is not covered by your plan, please contact us. We can discuss your needs and provide a quote for additional services.

-What's your experience for providing Website Maintenance services?

We have a development team of experienced WordPress specialists who keep your website secure, optimized and running smoothly. More information about our team and qualifications can be found on the "About Us" page. For any specific query you can reach us at contact@arwebcrafts.com.

-Why should I trust you to take care of my WordPress site?

We understand the importance of your website security and are committed to providing reliable and secure maintenance services. We offer a transparent service with clear communication and our priority is the health and performance of your website.

- How does a WordPress maintenance service improve site security?

At AR WebCrafts, we take several steps to increase security. These include regular updates, monitoring security for vulnerabilities, scanning for malware, and implementing best practices to prevent unauthorized access.

- What happens if my site has already been hacked?

While our maintenance plans are proactive and help prevent hackers, if your site has already been compromised, we offer site cleaning services (this may require an additional fee depending on the severity).
