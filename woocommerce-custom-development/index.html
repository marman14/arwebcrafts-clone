Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Custom WooCommerce

Transparent answers to common questions about our WooCommerce engineering process, timelines, scaling, and ownership.

Why should I invest in custom WooCommerce development instead of standard plugins?

Out-of-the-box WooCommerce and commercial plugins cover about 70% of standard store operations. However, stacking 30+ off-the-shelf plugins creates database bloat, fatal update conflicts, slow cart response times, and checkout drop-offs. Custom WooCommerce development by AR Webcrafts gives you lightweight, purpose-built code tailored to your exact business model. Your checkout loads in sub-seconds, conflicts are eliminated, and you maintain complete control without recurring plugin license fees.

How do you prevent store crashes and slow checkouts during high-traffic flash sales?

High traffic bottlenecks in WooCommerce are usually caused by uncached cart sessions, unindexed database tables, and synchronous external API calls during checkout. We eliminate these bottlenecks by configuring High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS), setting up Redis object caching, optimizing custom MySQL indexes, and offloading heavy tasks (like email dispatch or ERP syncing) to asynchronous Action Scheduler queues.

Can you integrate WooCommerce with our ERP, CRM, or warehouse management system?

Yes. We build secure, bi-directional API bridges connecting WooCommerce with NetSuite, SAP, Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Fishbowl, ShipStation, and custom internal inventory ERPs. Our integrations feature robust error logging, automatic retry queues, and webhook listeners to ensure your inventory, orders, and customer data never desynchronize.

Will our business own 100% of the custom WooCommerce code you write?

Yes, absolutely 100%. Upon final milestone delivery, all custom code, plugins, database schemas, and documentation are entirely your intellectual property. We do not use encrypted code, proprietary licensing callbacks, or vendor lock-in. You receive the complete Git repository ready for your internal team or any developer to maintain.

How long does a custom WooCommerce development project take?

Project timelines depend on scope. A targeted custom feature (such as a 1-page checkout flow or custom payment gateway) typically takes 2–3 weeks. A complex B2B wholesale engine or custom ERP bridge generally spans 4–8 weeks. Comprehensive store rebuilds or high-concurrency scaling overhauls take 6–10 weeks. Every project has a clear, fixed-price milestone schedule with weekly Friday demos.

What post-launch support and warranty do you provide?

Every custom build includes 60 days of post-launch bug warranty and technical monitoring. Because production traffic frequently exposes edge cases not visible on staging, we remain actively engaged to resolve any issues immediately at zero extra charge. We also offer ongoing monthly maintenance retainers for continuous feature development and security updates.
