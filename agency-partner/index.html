FAQ

Answers to Your White Label Questions

Will AR Webcrafts ever contact my clients directly?

Never. We have a strict policy of zero direct client contact. All communication goes through you. We sign NDAs as standard and our name never appears in any deliverable unless you specifically ask us to.

How do you handle invoicing and payments?

We invoice you (the agency) directly. You handle your own invoicing with your client and keep whatever margin you choose. We never know — or care — what you charge your client.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Standard websites: 5–10 business days. Complex web apps: 3–8 weeks. Mobile apps: 6–14 weeks. SaaS platforms: 8–20 weeks. We provide exact timelines in our quotes and stick to them.

What's your revision and quality guarantee?

Every project includes 2 rounds of revisions. We don't consider a project done until you're 100% satisfied. We also provide a 30-day bug fix warranty after delivery at no additional cost.

Can you work in our project management tools?

Yes. We work in Asana, ClickUp, Monday.com, Trello, Jira, Notion, or whatever you use. We adapt to your workflow, not the other way around. We can also set up a private Slack channel.

Do you work with agencies outside the US?

Absolutely. We partner with agencies across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe, and beyond. We have team members covering multiple time zones and we're accustomed to remote-first, async-friendly partnerships.
